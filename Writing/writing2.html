Writing Exercise 2

Q: What is a human-made creation that brings out the best in you? Why?

It could be an object, tool, work of art, process, system, environment… or something else human-made. What positive qualities does this creation call forth in you? What positive state(s) does it awaken in you? How does it empower you to express your value and strengths? Why does it have this effect on you? Rather than focusing on tools that augment your productivity or performance on instrumental tasks, pay attention to how human-made things uplift you and allow you to be the best version of yourself.

Music is always something that brings out the best in me. It doesn't matter the genre [unless its country which I can't stand] it will always improve my mood. Even sad music is healing. Whenever I need to prepare for something big such as a presentation I listen to music, or even something small as well. I find that it is a mental reset and comforting. It is also such a big way to connect and I find myself making friends with people who enjoy similar songs/albums/etc. I become a better person because I feel better and I think that is really important. As a more shy person anything that brings me confidence and comfot is something that helps me thrive. I really like to listen to physical media because it helps ground me as well. I am not someone who sticks to an album so being forced to hear the story that the artist is trying to tell is good.
