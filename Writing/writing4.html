Writing Exercise 4

Q: What is something you appreciate or long for from the past? Why?

It could be something you lived through, or something from the distant past. It might be a tradition or an antiquated value that you appreciate, an old way of doing things that you prefer / are fond of, or even a personal time or cultural moment that you hope to reconnect with. What do you appreciate about it? What wisdom does it contain? What can it teach us about living well today?

One of the times I often miss was when I was a big reader. While it is still a pleasure of mine, I often get distracted with other things or too overwhelmed. I think I was a much strong writer during that time of my life and the continous knowledge of words served me well. I think that reading is extremamly important and the less I do, it becomes noticable. It also allows you to escape reality in a way that I find very much needed. I honestly do think that my phone is to blame as I spend all my free time on it. I also chose it as a go to escape instead of healthier methods such as books. I try and set goals with myself often to try and get back to reading but I think it is honestly a skill. It takes a lot of patience and focus that I find hard to gather nowadays.
