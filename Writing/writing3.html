Writing Exercise 3

Q: When do you cherish the slow or hard way of doing something? Why?

When do you embrace constraints or prefer inefficiency? When do you willingly sacrifice convenience or comfort? What does the limited, slower, or more effortful approach offer that the quicker and easier way does not? What can we learn from this about human fulfillment and the kinds of technologies that might support it?

One of the first things that comes to mind is shopping. While I am not the biggest fan in general, I always prefer going into a store and looking through everything and trying things on over an online order. I think nowadays its so easy to overconsume due to the ease of shopping and being able to find anything within a few clicks. I think the aspect of searching and finding something that you know works for you is way more exciting and it is a way to keep consumption down. It is definetetly a sacrifice of comfort as I get overwhelmed shopping quickly and never end the day without a breakdown. It also makes me think more about what I am actually willing to spend money on because the impact feels greater when you are in person. The ability to click a button has ruined a lot of things and I think that consumption and human thoughtfullness is one of them.
