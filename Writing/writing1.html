Writing Exercise 1

Q: What is a Ritual, Practice or Routine that is important for wellbeing and/ or fulfillment? Why?

How does this ritual or behavior contribute to your life? What aspects of it makes it valuable to you? What can it teach us about the nature or ingredients of human fulfillment more broadly?

One practice near and dear to my heart is going on drives. I like to do this whenever I am overwhelemed or am in need of a smile. It allows me to get fresh air and open my eyes a bit beyond wherever I am feeling stuck or cramped. I also enjoy it because I have to be hyperfocused on driving and mind is not allowed to wander. It always makes me smile and the sound of the engine is cathartic. It also makes me think of my dad as this is a ritual we started together. When I think beyond just this ritual and my appreciation for cars, there is also a community behind it. It has allowed me to meet so many new people and make special connections, even if it is someone who I normally wouldn't interact with. Going back to the driving aspect, I think of all the times my friends and I woudl go for a drive at night or just for fun. It's a way to listen to music, have social interaction, but not feel pressured into anything. In general it has always made me fufilled and I think its because it is so many things at once.
