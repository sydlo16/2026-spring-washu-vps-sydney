Do Artifacts Have Ethics?

While I think that ethics are always important to discuss and consider, I don't think we can let questions like this control innovation. There are many technologies and inventions that have emerged over time that would not respond well to these questions, such as : What practices will the use of this technology displace? However if humans limit their creation it will hold us back as a society. Instead we need to ask more questions in response and figure out a plan to help aid issues that are being created.
